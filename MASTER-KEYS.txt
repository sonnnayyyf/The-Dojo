Key/code management has moved to private-tools/ (see private-tools/README.md).

- Content keys: private-tools/keys/<course>.ck  (never publish)
- Issued-code ledger: private-tools/ledger.csv  (never publish)

To lock a finished course:   node private-tools/lock-course.mjs <course-id>
To issue a student a code:   node private-tools/issue-code.mjs <course-id> "<student label>"
